VAT rates in Nordhavn

Nordhavn has two VAT rates: a standard rate of 24% and a reduced rate of 12%. There is no zero rate for domestic sales.

RatePercentageApplies to
Standard24%Most goods and services
Reduced12%Food, hotel stays, newspapers
