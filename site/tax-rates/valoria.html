VAT rates in Valoria

Valoria has three VAT rates: a standard rate of 21%, a reduced rate of 8% and a zero rate. Most goods and services use the standard rate.

RatePercentageApplies to
Standard21%Most goods and services
Reduced8%Food, books, public transport
Zero0%Exports, medical supplies
