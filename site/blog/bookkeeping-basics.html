Bookkeeping basics for freelancers

Bookkeeping is the habit of recording every euro that comes in and goes out of your business, with a receipt or invoice to prove it. Good books make your tax return quick and show you whether you are actually making money.

Which records should you keep?

  • Every invoice you send
  • Every receipt for a business expense
  • Your bank statements

A 15-minute weekly routine

  1. Photograph new receipts.
  2. Match bank transactions to receipts and invoices.
  3. Follow up on unpaid invoices older than 14 days.
