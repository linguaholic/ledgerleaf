Invoice checklist: 9 things every invoice must include

A valid invoice names both parties, has a unique number and date, describes what you delivered, and shows the net amount, VAT rate, VAT amount and total. Missing details are the most common reason invoices are paid late.

  1. Your name and address
  2. Your client's name and address
  3. Your tax or VAT number
  4. A unique, sequential invoice number
  5. The invoice date
  6. The delivery date or service period
  7. A description of the goods or services
  8. Net amount, VAT rate and VAT amount
  9. The total amount due and payment terms
