Year-end closing checklist for small businesses

Closing your books means making sure every transaction of the year is recorded and correct, so your annual accounts and tax return are based on final numbers.

  1. Reconcile every bank and card account to the 31 December statement.
  2. Count stock and check outstanding invoices.
  3. Review unusual expenses and missing receipts.
  4. Adjust for prepaid and accrued costs.
  5. Back up your records and lock the period.
